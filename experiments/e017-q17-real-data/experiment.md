# Q17 prediction versus decision utility on Hyperliquid

**State experiments:** E-017, E-267  
**Execution status:** completed

## Research question

- **Q-001:** Do the Q16-Q18 findings survive protocol-documented transfer to real Hyperliquid observations?

## Hypothesis

- **H-002:** Forecast-score rankings can disagree with matched trading-decision rankings on Hyperliquid under causal evaluation.

## Input

Thirty admitted native on-change BBO contracts covering BTC and ETH, fixed causal feature/target rules, and the original Q17 comparison families.

## Method

Fit the frozen forecasting families and compare forecast ranking with matched policy-cost ranking under supported event and receipt windows.

## Output

The scoped empirical adaptation completed 22 fits. Its joint forecast/decision ranking-reversal rule had zero passing model families.

## Findings

- **R-007:** Q17 original negative preserved and declared linear causal pipeline supported in synthetic software checks.
- **R-011:** Reviewed sampled BTC pilot: MLP macro-F1 advantage absent; scheduling loss alone does not establish original forecast/decision ranking reversal.
- **R-018:** Author reports failed joint forecasting/utility criterion across eight fixed periods and two assets; independent review pending. Full-day not_evaluable and conditional December empirical admission false.
- **R-037:** valid_completed_scoped_empirical_adaptation
