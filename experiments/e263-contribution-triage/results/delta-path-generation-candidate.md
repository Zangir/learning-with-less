## What the observable task actually permits

At forecast time t, the model receives only delivered, time-aligned sampled top-K L2 histories, the declared sampling/missingness mask and lawful metadata available by t. The target is a future sampled book window `S_(t+1:t+H)`. Its training label becomes available after that window has arrived; any future-derived event label is available at the same later time. All models may derive identical labels from these windows. No L3 order identities, hidden queue positions, participant labels or matched lifecycle history are assumed available.

Eligible tasks are functionals of the **sampled** future, such as the order of a spread-widening threshold and a top-K depth-depletion threshold, jointly with terminal mid-price direction. The label must state how same-bin ties, missing observations, truncated levels and right censoring are handled. A crossing between samples is not observed. A snapshot depth drop cannot by itself identify a cancellation, execution, spoofing action or the fill of a hypothetical order. A passive path generator also does not identify the response to a new trading action.

Book constraints can enforce correctly sorted prices, a valid spread under the admitted session rules, positive displayed-level quantities, permitted tick/lot grids and consistent reconstruction from coordinates. These are source-specific: absent levels and padding need masks, tick rules may depend on price, and an allowed locked state cannot be rejected because another venue disallows it. Diffusion time is an artificial noise coordinate, not market time; feasible final books do not imply that intermediate noisy states should obey book constraints.

There is no general volume-conservation law between two sampled snapshots unless additions, cancellations, trades, replenishment and depth entering/leaving the top-K window are observed or explicitly modeled. Under an unrestricted insert/cancel mechanism, many pairs of legal endpoint books can be connected by some unobserved events. Requiring bounded increments, fixed cumulative flow or smooth prices would add assumptions rather than certify missing history. This candidate therefore concerns legal sampled-book geometry and a learned joint distribution, not restored lifecycle truth.

Identification requires an admitted observation operator and a forecasting law that can be estimated across comparable histories. Constraint satisfaction alone does not identify conditional probabilities. A hypothetical scenario condition can be user-specified; its empirical frequency must not be confused with a causal intervention or supplied as actual future knowledge in a forecast test. No new project data eligibility, label access or stationarity claim is established by this memo.

## Mechanism audit and decisive counterexamples

The most concrete presently expressible proposal is:

```text
clean_estimate = denoise(noisy_path, history)
corrected = argmin_u distance(u, clean_estimate) + penalty(book_constraints(u))
next_noisy_path = diffusion_update(noisy_path, corrected)
```

This is the CPS pattern with book-specific constraints. Projecting the noisy path instead is the PDM pattern. Adjusting categorical probabilities to satisfy rules is covered by CDD; faster flow integration is already tested for L2 by FlowLOB. A gap/volume coordinate transformation, an existing constrained sampler and additional joint-event metrics do not specify a new update rule. **No distinct mechanism has been supplied, so the present candidate fails its first gate.**

The following are local elementary counterexamples, not newly claimed literature theorems:

- **Validity and marginal realism do not establish the joint law.** Fix bid 100 and ask 101. Let the bid depth be either 1 or 3, with all other displayed quantities fixed and valid. Model A assigns equal probability to two-step depth paths `(1,1)` and `(3,3)`; model B to `(1,3)` and `(3,1)`. Every snapshot is valid and each time marginal agrees. Yet the sampled event “depth falls from 3 to 1” has probability 0 under A and 1/2 under B. Thus a decision threshold of 1/4 separates the models despite perfect marginal agreement.
- **Projection need not preserve a conditional law.** If X is standard normal, projecting to nonnegative values gives `max(0,X)`, with a probability-1/2 atom at zero. The conditional distribution `X | X>=0` has no atom there. Applied blindly to volume, clipping can manufacture depletion probability even while fixing invalid signs. This does not prove all projection samplers fail; it disproves inference from feasibility alone to distributional calibration.
- **Reconstruction cannot create information.** For any generated path law q and observable task f, the readout `g_f(x)=E_q[f(S)|x]` is a direct predictor using exactly the same information. A direct implementation may share the same q or approximate its readout by distillation. Any surviving advantage must concern finite computation, regularization or acquisition, not additional test-time information. Closed r7/r8 equivalences remain closed.

## Matched controls and the missing result

The missing result would be a specific sampler that improves **joint-event probability accuracy at a measured end-to-end budget**, while preserving admitted book support, against the following information-matched alternatives:

1. Direct joint-event prediction with a shared causal encoder and the same primitive/path-derived labels, selection budget and lawful metadata. Independent scalar heads alone are insufficient when the claim concerns joint events.
2. A shared autoregressive or other joint path-distribution model in valid coordinates; a simple conditional block-resampling baseline; and a matched flow/diffusion backbone. Every output representation receives the same supplied book rules.
3. The same trained generator with no correction, final correction, PDM/CPS-style iterative correction and the proposed operator. This isolates the sampler from training capacity and representation changes.
4. A distilled task readout of the same path model, with teacher generation and distillation cost charged. For new queries, disclose whether they were known during training. Include a direct shared distribution or sufficient task basis, so new query support is not restricted by design.

For a finite declared family of path events, a direct model can predict the probability of each mutually exclusive joint outcome cell. Any later decision formed from that cell partition is only a readout. A genuinely new event may require a finer partition; both sides must be allowed the same refinement or shared generative state. Unlimited arbitrary future queries cannot be used to justify an unmeasured advantage over an artificially fixed direct head.

The primary endpoint should be a proper score, for example multiclass Brier score for a frozen joint signature containing first sampled threshold event, tie/none status and terminal price direction. Compare this at a specified latency or total-cost cap, or report the Pareto frontier. Use independent day/block uncertainty with target-window purging. Supplementary endpoints: complete-path constraint violation rate, rare joint-event calibration, a multivariate path score, diversity and relevant conditional distribution diagnostics. [LOB-Bench, ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/nagy25a.html) already provides conditional/unconditional book statistics and dependence-related metrics; use its applicable components rather than claiming generic realism evaluation as new.

Total cost includes normalization, training and all selection trials, sampler/constraint-solver calls, rejection attempts, decoding, Monte Carlo sample count, memory, wall latency and repeated queries. NFE alone misses potentially expensive projection and particle operations. Compare common hardware, batches and task count; charge shared training once and state the amortization workload. For a binary event estimated from M independent generated paths, Monte Carlo variance is `p(1-p)/M`, even before model bias. A direct probability head does not pay that sampling variance. Zero observed violations is not a universal feasibility certificate, and better proper scores are not trading profit.

## Smallest future distinguishing gate and kill criteria

**No empirical run is recommended now.** First require pseudocode for an operator not reducible to the mechanisms above and a proof obligation: exactly which constraints it satisfies, what distribution it targets, and how approximation bias and work scale. If these cannot be specified, stop with this no-go.

Only after that gate, the smallest authorized follow-up would be an exact, finite toy distribution over two legal books and four-step paths, with known conditional and joint-event probabilities. It can expose mode loss, projection-induced boundary mass and hidden rejection cost without a trained model or native matching engine. Compare the proposed operator against an exact sampler and the relevant existing correction on the same declared target. This is a future sampler test, not an execution performed here. Any later data test needs an admitted existing L2 source and separate training/compute authorization; the current 2-CPU/8-GiB preparation allowance does not make large published backbones feasible.

Kill the candidate if it has no distinct update rule or result; uses realized future regimes as forecasting inputs; depends on unobserved lifecycle labels; enforces unsupported temporal rules; improves only legality or marginals while worsening joint scores; beats only weak independent heads; loses its frontier after solver, sampling, distillation or tuning costs; or derives its claimed novelty entirely from combining a published L2 generator with a published constrained sampler.
