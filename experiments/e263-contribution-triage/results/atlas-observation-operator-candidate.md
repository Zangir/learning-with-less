## Information contract and source boundary

Let S_t denote the already recorded sampled-L2 history ending at decision cut t. It is the finest *available recorded view*, not the exchange’s complete order history. A known causal transformation O gives V_t = O(S_t); its operator identifier, retained-field mask, observation timestamps, ages and past operator changes are supplied identically to every deployed learner. Outcomes Y_t are obtained later from the same recorded source on a prespecified fixed clock. Models never receive discarded fields, later packets, interpolation using future samples, or fine-view teacher outputs at test time.

During training, every arm receives identical labeled histories, lawful operator metadata and generated pairs (O(S_t), O'(S_t)). If an auxiliary objective uses the original recorded view, the direct competitors receive that view for training too. Operator changes are prespecified or randomized independently of the outcome for the controlled test. A policy triggered by unobserved market activity, an unknown provider sampling mechanism, changing exchange tick rules, and ordinary market drift are different mechanisms; knowing a resolution setting does not identify them.

The accepted RV029 report admits integrity-bound normalized BTC top-five inputs and their one-second as-of consumer behavior for four later dates. It explicitly leaves provider sampling, exchange completeness and publication latency unverified; release/admission timestamps are null. Therefore depth truncation and thinning of retained history are reproducible perturbations of those normalized inputs. They are **not** authenticated simulations of a different live feed, an exchange-latency experiment, or a source of queue/fill/lifecycle truth. This memo reads the accepted report, not the raw stream, and does not extend its admission. The later dates are exposed, not pristine holdouts; their negative depth result motivates caution, not proof that depth lacks information.

## Identification and a decisive counterexample

These are elementary derivations for triage, not proposed novel theorems.

If the recorded joint law of (S,Y) is fixed, O is selected independently and O is known, the target probability is p_o(v) = P(Y=1 | O(S)=v). A changed mixture over the same supported operators reweights their risks. It does not change those operator-specific conditional probabilities. Training on the same transformed labeled pairs therefore gives direct and structured learners the same identifiable target. An unsupported operator or a change in P(S,Y) requires additional assumptions; a mask token does not supply them.

For nested views C = a(F), the correct Bayes relation is

    p_C(C) = E[p_F(F) | C].

It is generally **not** p_C(C) = p_F(F) for every paired realization. For example, take H ~ Bernoulli(1/2), Y = H, F = H and C = a constant. Fine prediction has Bayes Brier risk zero; coarse prediction must be 1/2 and has risk 1/4. Exact equality of fine and coarse representations, when the prediction head has no bypass carrying H, forces the fine prediction to be constant too. Its best Brier risk becomes 1/4. An operator identifier does not rescue the discarded value. A soft consistency penalty may trade bias against variance, but has no generic no-harm guarantee. This counterexample is a condition for designing the objective, not a refutation of every finite-weight consistency method.

If a fixed fine teacher is trained using only training data and its output T(F) is used as a squared-error soft target, the unrestricted coarse optimum is E[T(F)|C]. That is ordinary conditional regression/distillation. If the teacher is Bayes-correct, the tower relation gives p_C; if not, its conditional bias remains. A “coherent representation” proposal must identify a gain beyond that matched direct control rather than treating the tower identity as new.

## Smallest discriminating future test, conditional on separate authorization

This is a falsifiable engineering test specification, not a recommendation to execute another sweep. Its narrow hypothesis is: **at a fixed training and inference budget, the proposed operator-aware consistency objective improves worst-operator prediction risk beyond direct augmentation and existing consistency/distillation controls, without degrading the finest-view risk.** No new algorithm is currently specified beyond those controls, so the novelty gate fails before execution.

1. Use only integrity-bound existing top-five normalized histories. Freeze contiguous train/validation/test blocks before generating views, purge history/target overlap, and acknowledge prior date exposure. At each fixed one-second cut, retain a shared current best bid/ask for all arms. Define one binary sampled-midpoint endpoint at t+10 seconds; equality is its own prespecified outcome convention, not silently dropped. This measures a recorded-price event, not profit or fill probability. All arms share the same segment/support eligibility and target timestamps.
2. Freeze a small known family: depth truncation to 1, 2 or 5 levels and retention of older history at every first or fourth original cut, with timestamps/masks explicit. Keep current top-of-book unchanged to preserve the decision anchor. Evaluate the six resulting operators on the same later rows; never invent intra-sample states or bridge disconnects. An additional depth/history combination may be withheld only if explicitly labeled extrapolation, with no guaranteed identification claim. No parameter or period selection uses the final evaluation.
3. Compare five learners with the same backbone, parameter allowance, causal window, transformed examples and operator metadata: direct supervised augmentation; direct augmentation with worst-operator loss weighting; a MIRRAMS-style consistency adaptation; coarse direct regression on fine-teacher probabilities; and the proposed method. A teacher is a sixth fitted object and its training cost counts. Two frozen seeds give a hard ceiling of twelve fits, with no hyperparameter sweep. Any claimed new component must be stated mathematically before this comparison can be frozen.
4. Primary endpoint: maximum across the six operator-specific mean Brier losses, with days weighted equally within each operator. Report all operator/day losses, the finest-view loss, fixed training-frequency reference, and measured total training/inference costs. A prospective practical margin could be 0.002 absolute Brier, fixed before outcomes; it is a proposed engineering threshold, not an existing economic threshold. Use paired day/block uncertainty and include seed variation; an interval too wide to distinguish the margin is inconclusive, not a positive result or automatic falsification.

Kill the *contribution* claim if its objective is merely the comparator under another parameterization, a gain disappears when operators/views/teacher access and total costs are matched, the practical margin is excluded by a suitably powered paired interval, or its benefit requires inappropriate pointwise invariance that degrades informative fine views. Stop operationally if existing dates cannot support a defensible split or sufficient independent blocks. A positive result on exposed dates would justify only an engineering follow-up; it would not establish an ICLR contribution, live feed robustness or untouched-holdout confirmation.

Before any execution, cap the data, total CPU time and memory in a separate protocol based on actual existing row counts and implementation cost. The twelve-fit ceiling is an upper bound, not a runtime estimate or authorization to consume the resource budget. This literature-only task does not acquire additional data to make the test feasible.
