# Q18 depth, history, and delay factorial on Hyperliquid

**State experiments:** E-018, E-268  
**Execution status:** completed  
**Scientific assessment:** hour00_and_full_day_limited_descriptive_negatives_accepted_mandatory_empirical_open, valid

## Research question

- **Q-001:** Do the Q16-Q18 findings survive protocol-documented transfer to real Hyperliquid observations?

## Hypothesis

- **H-003:** The incremental value of book depth depends on history and action delay, with interactions estimable under fixed chronological splits.

## Input

Eighteen admitted sampled-L2 receipt-clock contracts across BTC and ETH with fixed chronological roles and common supported rows.

## Method

Run a frozen factorial comparison over depth, history, and delayed views while keeping normalization and transformations training-only.

## Output

The exploratory adaptation completed 120 fits. No fixed common crossing supported a universal depth/history/delay benefit; effects were heterogeneous and asset-dependent.

## Findings

- **R-008:** Q18 rebuilt common-view and training-only pipeline supported; guard/schema integration requires correction.
- **R-010:** Reviewed mixed depth/history contrasts in one sampled BTC evaluation hour; no useful general benefit, noninferiority or original-primary success established.
- **R-017:** Author reports failed descriptive cross-asset freshness gate in32 fixed fits; independent model-result review pending. Original strict panel and full-day scope separate.
- **R-019:** Independently accepted RV017: failed fixed descriptive freshness criterion in exact RV013 full-day adaptation, including absolute constant-action baselines. Mandatory original real-data outcome remains open.
